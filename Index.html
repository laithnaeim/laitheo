Privacy Policy

A starting policy for Laitheo. It will be updated as new features are added.

What we collect

If you create an account we store your name, email address and profile photo from your sign-in method (Google or email). When you order we use the details you share to fulfil it.

How we use it

Data is used only to run your account, process and follow up on orders. We do not sell customer information.

Storage

Account data is stored securely with Google Firebase. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

Returns

Unopened physical items can be exchanged within 3 days. Opened products, activated subscriptions and delivered services are not refundable.
